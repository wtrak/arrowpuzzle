Select a square
Tap an empty square, then choose its arrow.
Solved! Every row, column, and dot satisfies the rules.
Black cells are starting clues. The side counters are tutorial aids; they can disappear from the eventual game.

How to play

Fill every white square with one diagonal arrow. The arrows do not make a path; each arrow simply chooses one corner of its square.

1

Exactly one arrow points at every dot

Each interior dot touches four squares. Exactly one of those four arrows must point toward that dot.

↘↗↙↘
2

Balance every row

Each 4-cell row has exactly 2 left arrows (↖ ↙) and 2 right arrows (↗ ↘).

3

Balance every column

Each column has exactly 2 up arrows (↖ ↗) and 2 down arrows (↙ ↘).

How a move becomes forced: a row can tell you LEFT/RIGHT, a column can tell you UP/DOWN, and a nearby dot can rule out a corner. Combine those restrictions until one arrow remains.
