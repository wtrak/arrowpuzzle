Four directions. One solution.

A language-free logic puzzle. Balance every row and column, and make sure exactly one arrow points toward every interior dot.

How EQUARROW works

↖ ↗ ↙ ↘
1

One arrow per dot. Every interior dot must have exactly one of its four neighboring arrows pointing toward it.

2

Balance every row. On a 4×4 board, exactly two arrows point left and two point right.

3

Balance every column. Exactly two arrows point up and two point down.
